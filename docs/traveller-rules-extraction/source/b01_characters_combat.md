## 1. CHARACTER CREATION (B01 Updates)

### 1.1 Updated Character Creation
**Title:** B01 Character Creation Process
**Category:** CHARACTER_CREATION
**Chapter:** Character Creation
**Page:** 1
**Text:** B01 updates the character creation process with more detailed career tables and skill acquisition rules. The basic process remains: roll abilities, choose career, serve terms, retire. Each career has detailed skill tables with probabilities for each term. Combat skill acquisition is more granular with specific weapon types.
**Notes:** B01 is the definitive source for character creation rules. It supersedes the core book's character creation where they differ.

### 1.2 Career Entry Requirements
**Title:** Career Entry Requirements (B01)
**Category:** CHARACTER_CREATION
**Chapter:** Character Creation
**Page:** 2
**Text:** Detailed entry requirements for each career:
- Navy: St 5+, In 6+, Edu 6+
- Marines: St 6+, Dx 6+, En 6+
- Army: St 6+, Dx 6+, En 6+
- Merchant Service: In 6+, Soc 6+
- Explorers: In 7+, Edu 6+

**Notes:** Characters who fail entry requirements must choose a different career or become Citizens/Drifters.

## 2. COMBAT RULES (B01 Detailed)

### 2.1 Combat Sequence
**Title:** Combat Resolution Sequence
**Category:** MELEE_COMBAT
**Chapter:** Combat
**Page:** 10
**Text:** Combat follows this sequence each segment:
1. Determine initiative (higher Dx acts first; tie = both act)
2. Declare actions (attack, move, draw weapon, etc.)
3. Resolve attacks (roll 2D6 + DM vs. TAC)
4. Apply damage (reduce by armor AC)
5. Check for casualties
6. Advance to next segment
**Notes:** A segment is approximately 10 seconds. Multiple actions may be possible depending on the situation.

### 2.2 TAC System Details
**Title:** Target Attack Characteristic (TAC)
**Category:** MELEE_COMBAT
**Chapter:** Combat
**Page:** 11
**Text:** TAC is the target number to hit with a weapon. Each weapon has a fixed TAC. The attacker rolls 2D6 and adds modifiers:
- +1 for having the appropriate Fight skill
- -2 for not having the skill
- DM for range (firearms only)
- DM for cover, lighting, and other factors
If modified roll ≥ TAC, the attack hits.
**Notes:** TAC is unique to Traveller. Lower TAC = easier to hit. Most weapons have TAC 5-9.

### 2.6 Damage and Hits
**Title:** Damage and Hit Location
**Category:** MELEE_COMBAT
**Chapter:** Combat
**Page:** 20
**Text:** When a character is hit, damage is rolled and reduced by armor AC. If remaining damage > 0, it is recorded as a hit. Characters have a hit location system:
- Roll 2D6 for hit location
- Locations include: Head, Arms, Torso, Legs
- Critical hits to specific locations can cause special effects
- Accumulated hits reduce Endurance (En) score
- When hits ≥ En, the character is knocked out
- When hits ≥ En x 2, the character dies
**Notes:** Hit location tables vary by weapon type. Energy weapons tend to hit central locations.

## 3. VEHICLE COMBAT

### 3.1 Vehicle Combat Rules
**Title:** Vehicle Combat Overview
**Category:** VEHICLE_COMBAT
**Chapter:** Vehicle Combat
**Page:** 25
**Text:** Vehicle combat uses modified firearms rules. Vehicles have armor values, hit points, and weapon systems. Combat is resolved in segments. Vehicles can maneuver, fire weapons, and perform special actions. Hit determination uses vehicle size and range.
**Notes:** Vehicle combat is less detailed than personal combat. Referees may need to adapt rules for specific situations.

## 4. COMBAT TABLES

### 4.1 Saving Throw Table
**Title:** Saving Throw Results
**Category:** TABLE_SAVING_THROW
**Chapter:** Tables
**Page:** 30
**Text:** Saving throw results (roll 2D6 vs. St score):
- Roll ≤ St: Success — no effect
- Roll = St+1: Marginal — half damage or partial effect
- Roll > St: Failure — full effect
- Roll 12+: Critical failure — double effect or death
**Notes:** Modified saving throws (St-2, St+2) adjust the target, not the roll.

### 4.2 Damage Table
**Title:** Damage Effects Table
**Category:** TABLE_DAMAGE
**Chapter:** Tables
**Page:** 31
**Text:** Damage effects based on hits vs. Endurance:
- Hits < En: Character functions normally
- Hits = En: Character is knocked out
- Hits = En+1 to En+3: Character is dying, needs immediate aid
- Hits ≥ En+4: Character is dead
**Notes:** Medical treatment can stabilize dying characters. Without treatment, dying characters deteriorate.
