# B03: Worlds and Adventures — Rules Extraction

## Source
Classic Traveller Book 3: Worlds and Adventures (1981), GDW

## Contents

### Star Mapping (Page 4)
- Subsectors: 8x10 hex grids (1 parsec per hex)
- World occurrence: 4+ on 1D6
- Starport type: 2D6 (2-3=A, 4-5=B, 6-8=C, 9-10=D, 11-12=E, 12=None)
- Bases: Based on starport type
- Gas giants: 10+ on 2D6
- Travel zones: Amber (caution), Red (danger)
- Communications routes connect key worlds

### World Creation (Page 6)
- UWP: 10-digit code
- Starport, size, atmosphere, hydrographics, population, government, law, TL
- Population in millions, world income
- Trade codes derived from UWP

### Technological Level (Page 7)
- **TL0**: Pre-industrial
- **TL1-3**: Early industrial
- **TL4-5**: Industrial
- **TL6**: Modern, computers
- **TL7**: Fusion, basic spaceflight
- **TL8**: Advanced spaceflight, lasers
- **TL9**: Jump drive, full spacefaring
- **TL10**: AI, power armor
- **TL11**: Advanced materials
- **TLA**: Unknown advanced

### Trade Classifications (Page 16)
Ag, Po, Ri, Ve, Fl, Lo, Hi, Wa, Co, Bu, Ex, Va, Th, Ch, Ce, Nc, Sc, Xc, Nz, Re, Cr, Gz, It, Oc, Ph, Tu, Yu, Zo

### Equipment and Vehicles (Page 17)
- Personal equipment: Weapons, armor, tools, shelters
- Personal devices: Computers, communicators, sensors
- Vision aids: Night vision, telescopic
- Vehicles: Cars, trucks, helicopters, boats
- Costs scale with TL

### Random Encounters (Page 24)
- Urban, wilderness, and space encounter tables
- Reaction rolls determine NPC attitude
- Combat, trade, or social interactions

### Patron Encounters (Page 25)
- Types: Government, corporate, criminal, personal
- Each patron has mission type, reward, deadline
- Reaction rolls determine attitude

### Animal Encounters (Page 28)
- Tables based on planet type and environment
- Animals have: Size, movement, armor, damage, number
- Combat uses standard TAC system
- Tables generated per world

### Psionics System (Page 38)
- Mental powers for rare individuals
- Strength determined by In score
- Training at Psionics Institute required
- Psionic use causes fatigue

### Psionic Disciplines (Page 40)
1. **Telepathy**: Mind reading, mental attacks, illusion
2. **Clairvoyance**: Remote viewing, scrying
3. **Telekinesis**: Moving objects, force blasts
4. **Awareness**: Detecting life, psionics, thoughts
5. **Teleportation**: Short-range movement
6. **Special**: Time manipulation, probability alteration
