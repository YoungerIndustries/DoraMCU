## 1. MILITARY ORGANIZATION

### 1.1 Army Structure
**Title:** Imperial Army Organization
**Category:** MILITARY_ORGANIZATION
**Chapter:** Army
**Page:** 1
**Text:** The Imperial Army is organized into standard units:
- Squad: 10 soldiers, led by a Corporal
- Section: 2 squads (20 soldiers), led by a Sergeant
- Platoon: 3 sections (60 soldiers), led by a Lieutenant
- Company: 4 platoons (240 soldiers), led by a Major
- Battalion: 4 companies (960 soldiers), led by a Colonel
- Regiment: 3 battalions (2880 soldiers), led by a General
**Notes:** Each unit has support elements (medics, engineers, communications) not counted in the base numbers.

### 1.2 Marine Structure
**Title:** Imperial Marines Organization
**Category:** MILITARY_ORGANIZATION
**Chapter:** Marines
**Page:** 5
**Text:** Imperial Marines are organized similarly to Army but with space combat capabilities:
- Fire Team: 4 marines
- Squad: 2 fire teams (8 marines)
- Platoon: 3 squads (24 marines)
- Company: 4 platoons (96 marines)
- Battalion: 3 companies (288 marines)
Marines specialize in boarding actions and planetary assaults.
**Notes:** Marines are elite troops with better equipment and training than Army.

### 1.3 Navy Structure
**Title:** Imperial Navy Organization
**Category:** MILITARY_ORGANIZATION
**Chapter:** Navy
**Page:** 10
**Text:** The Imperial Navy organizes ships into formations:
- Division: 2-4 ships
- Squadron: 3-6 divisions (6-24 ships)
- Task Force: 2-4 squadrons (12-96 ships)
- Fleet: 2-4 task forces (24-384 ships)
Each formation is commanded by an officer of appropriate rank.
**Notes:** Navy formations vary widely in composition. Capital ships anchor task forces.

## 2. MILITARY EQUIPMENT

### 2.1 Military Weapons
**Title:** Military-Specific Weapons
**Category:** MILITARY_EQUIPMENT
**Chapter:** Equipment
**Page:** 15
**Text:** Military-specific weapons not available to civilians:
- Assault Rifle: TAC 6/7/8/9/10, 2D6 damage, 500m range
- Heavy Machine Gun: TAC 6/7/8/9/-, 3D6 damage, 1000m range
- Recoilless Rifle: TAC 7/8/9/10/-, 4D6 damage, 2000m range
- Flamethrower: TAC 4/5/6/-/-, 2D6 damage, 30m range
- Rocket Launcher: TAC 8/9/10/11/-, 6D6 damage, 3000m range
**Notes:** These weapons require military career or special access. Civilian possession is illegal on most worlds.

### 2.2 Military Armor
**Title:** Military Armor Systems
**Category:** MILITARY_EQUIPMENT
**Chapter:** Equipment
**Page:** 18
**Text:** Military-grade armor:
- Combat Armor: AC 4, Cost 5000cr, Weight 20kg
- Power Armor: AC 5, Cost 10000cr, Weight 30kg, requires TL9+
- Heavy Power Armor: AC 6, Cost 20000cr, Weight 50kg, requires TL10+
- Vehicle Armor: Varies by vehicle type
**Notes:** Military armor provides superior protection but is heavy and expensive.

### 2.3 Military Vehicles
**Title:** Military Ground Vehicles
**Category:** MILITARY_EQUIPMENT
**Chapter:** Equipment
**Page:** 20
**Text:** Standard military vehicles:
- Light Scout Car: 2 tons, AC 1, 1D6 weapon, 100km/h
- Armored Personnel Carrier: 20 tons, AC 3, 2D6 weapon, 60km/h
- Main Battle Tank: 50 tons, AC 5, 4D6 weapon, 40km/h
- Artillery Piece: 10 tons, AC 2, 6D6 weapon, 0km/h (towed)
**Notes:** Vehicles require trained crews. Fuel consumption is significant.

## 3. MILITARY TACTICS

### 3.1 Ground Combat Tactics
**Title:** Ground Combat Tactics
**Category:** MILITARY_TACTICS
**Chapter:** Tactics
**Page:** 25
**Text:** Ground combat tactics include:
- Assault: Direct attack on enemy position
- Defense: Hold position against enemy attack
- Ambush: Surprise attack from concealed position
- Flanking: Attack from the side or rear
- Feint: False attack to draw enemy response
- Retreat: Ordered withdrawal under fire
Each tactic has specific rules for resolution.
**Notes:** Tactics provide DM bonuses or penalties based on execution and terrain.

### 3.2 Boarding Actions
**Title:** Boarding Action Rules
**Category:** BOARDING_ACTION
**Chapter:** Tactics
**Page:** 30
**Text:** Boarding actions allow marines to attack enemy ships:
1. Boarding ship must be within grappling range
2. Marines launch in boarding shuttles or vacc suits
3. Boarding party attempts to breach hull
4. Once inside, marines fight through ship
5. Objective: capture bridge, destroy weapons, or plant explosives
Boarding is risky but can disable larger ships.
**Notes:** Boarding requires Star Marine branch and appropriate equipment.

## 4. MILITARY CAREERS

### 4.1 Army Career Details
**Title:** Army Career (B04)
**Category:** CAREER_ARMY
**Chapter:** Careers
**Page:** 35
**Text:** The Army career in detail:
- Entry: St 6+, Dx 6+, En 6+
- Branches: Infantry, Armor, Artillery, General
- Skills: Fight (Light Rifle), Fight (Heavy Gun), Drive Vehicle, Explosives, Gunner, Leadership, Recon, Stealth
- Promotions: Private → Corporal → Sergeant → Lieutenant → Captain → Major → Colonel → General
- Wealth: D6+4 at retirement
**Notes:** Army officers command ground forces. They are the backbone of planetary defense.

### 4.2 Marines Career Details
**Title:** Marines Career (B04)
**Category:** CAREER_MARINES
**Chapter:** Careers
**Page:** 38
**Text:** The Marines career in detail:
- Entry: St 6+, Dx 6+, En 6+
- Branches: Assault, Star Marine, General
- Skills: Fight skills, Gunner, Stealth, Recon, Vacc Suit, Zero-G
- Promotions: Private → Corporal → Sergeant → Lieutenant → Captain → Major → Colonel
- Wealth: D6+5 at retirement
**Notes:** Marines are elite combat troops. Star Marines specialize in space combat.
