## 1. ADVANCED SHIP DESIGN

### 1.1 High Guard Ship Design
**Title:** High Guard Ship Design System
**Category:** SHIP_DESIGN
**Chapter:** Ship Design
**Page:** 1
**Text:** High Guard expands ship design with more detailed rules for capital ships and warships:
- Hull types: Standard, Reinforced, Armored
- Weapon mounts: Fixed, Turreted, Battery
- Armor types: Standard, Reinforced, Ablative
- Point Defense systems with detailed interception rules
- Damage control teams and repair rules
- Ship size categories from 200 tons to 1,000,000+ tons
**Notes:** High Guard is the definitive source for naval combat. It supersedes B02 for warship design.

### 1.2 Capital Ship Design
**Title:** Capital Ship Construction
**Category:** SHIP_DESIGN
**Chapter:** Ship Design
**Page:** 5
**Text:** Capital ships (10,000+ tons) have special design rules:
- Multiple weapon batteries
- Extensive armor plating
- Damage control compartments
- Crew in the thousands
- Support ships and shuttles
- Cost: 100,000cr per ton base + components
**Notes:** Capital ships are the backbone of Imperial Navy fleets. They are extremely expensive.

### 1.3 Ship Armor (High Guard)
**Title:** Advanced Ship Armor
**Category:** SHIP_ARMOR
**Chapter:** Ship Design
**Page:** 10
**Text:** High Guard armor system:
- Standard Armor: 1000cr per point per 1000 tons
- Reinforced Armor: 2000cr per point per 1000 tons, +1 PD
- Ablative Armor: 1500cr per point per 1000 tons, self-repairing
- Armor reduces damage from all weapon types
- Point Defense (PD) intercepts missiles before impact
**Notes:** Armor selection is a key design tradeoff. More armor = less cargo space.

## 2. ADVANCED SPACE COMBAT

### 2.1 Fleet Combat
**Title:** Fleet Combat Rules
**Category:** SPACE_COMBAT
**Chapter:** Combat
**Page:** 15
**Text:** Fleet combat involves multiple ships fighting in formation:
- Formations: Line, Wedge, Screen, Circle
- Formation provides tactical bonuses
- Command and control between ships
- Fleet commander coordinates attacks
- Individual ship actions within formation constraints
**Notes:** Fleet combat is complex. Referees should use battle maps and miniatures.

### 2.2 Range Bands (High Guard)
**Title:** High Guard Range Bands
**Category:** SPACE_COMBAT
**Chapter:** Combat
**Page:** 18
**Text:** High Guard defines precise range bands:
- Point Blank: 0-1000 km
- Short: 1001-3000 km
- Medium: 3001-6000 km
- Long: 6001-12000 km
- Extreme: 12001+ km
Each weapon type has effective ranges. Beyond effective range, hits are impossible.
**Notes:** Range is measured in kilometers. Ship movement changes range each impulse.

### 2.3 Missile Combat
**Title:** Missile Combat Rules
**Category:** SPACE_COMBAT
**Chapter:** Combat
**Page:** 20
**Text:** Missile combat in detail:
- Missiles have independent targeting
- Point Defense can intercept missiles
- PD roll: 2D6 vs. PD target number
- Multiple missiles can target one ship
- Missile types: Standard, Warhead, Nuclear
- Missile cost: 1000cr-10000cr each
**Notes:** Missiles are the most destructive ship weapons but limited in number.

### 2.4 Damage Control
**Title:** Damage Control Rules
**Category:** SPACE_COMBAT
**Chapter:** Combat
**Page:** 25
**Text:** Damage control allows ships to repair systems during combat:
- Engineer skill check each impulse
- Success reduces damage by 1D6 points
- Multiple engineers can work on same system
- Critical damage requires multiple impulses to repair
- Ships can be scuttled to prevent capture
**Notes:** Damage control is essential for ship survival. Without it, ships accumulate fatal damage.

## 3. HIGH GUARD WEAPONS

### 3.1 Ship Lasers (High Guard)
**Title:** High Guard Ship Lasers
**Category:** SHIP_WEAPONS
**Chapter:** Weapons
**Page:** 30
**Text:** High Guard laser weapons:
- Light Laser: 1D6 damage, 1 ton, 1000cr
- Heavy Laser: 2D6 damage, 2 tons, 2000cr
- Superheavy Laser: 3D6 damage, 4 tons, 4000cr
- Lasers can be mounted in batteries (multiple tubes)
- Battery fire rolls separately for each tube
**Notes:** Laser batteries are the standard armament. More tubes = more damage potential.

### 3.2 Ship Missiles (High Guard)
**Title:** High Guard Ship Missiles
**Category:** SHIP_WEAPONS
**Chapter:** Weapons
**Page:** 32
**Text:** High Guard missile systems:
- Missile Launcher: 5 tons, 5000cr
- Standard Missile: 3D6 damage, 1 ton, 1000cr
- Warhead Missile: 4D6 damage, 1 ton, 2000cr
- Nuclear Missile: 6D6 damage, 2 tons, 10000cr
- Missile capacity varies by ship size
- Reload only possible at shipyard
**Notes:** Nuclear missiles are weapons of mass destruction. Their use is restricted by Imperial law.

## 4. NAVAL CAREERS

### 4.1 Navy Career (High Guard)
**Title:** Navy Career (B05)
**Category:** CAREER_NAVY
**Chapter:** Careers
**Page:** 35
**Text:** Navy career in High Guard detail:
- Entry: St 5+, In 6+, Edu 6+
- Branches: Space, Flight, General
- Space branch: Ship command, navigation, astrogation
- Flight branch: Aerospace fighter operations
- Skills: Astrogation, Navigation, Pilot, Sensors, Gunner, Engineer
- Promotions: Ensign → Lieutenant → Commander → Captain → Admiral
- Wealth: D6+6 at retirement
**Notes:** Navy officers command the Imperial fleet. Space branch is the most prestigious.

### 4.2 Naval Combat Skills
**Title:** Naval Combat Skills
**Category:** CAREER_NAVY
**Chapter:** Careers
**Page:** 38
**Text:** Key naval combat skills:
- Gunnery: Ship weapon operation, +1 DM to hit
- Sensors: Target detection and tracking
- Navigation: Ship movement and positioning
- Astrogation: Jump navigation
- Engineer: Ship system repair
- Leadership: Fleet command bonuses
**Notes:** Multiple skills combine for effective naval combat. Gunnery is the most important combat skill.
