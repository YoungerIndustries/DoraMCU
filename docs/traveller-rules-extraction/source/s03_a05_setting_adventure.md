## 1. SETTING: THE IMPERIUM

### 1.1 Third Imperium Overview
**Title:** The Third Imperium
**Category:** SETTING_IMPERIUM
**Chapter:** Setting
**Page:** 1
**Text:** The Third Imperium is a vast interstellar human empire spanning thousands of star systems. It is ruled by an Emperor from the capital world of Capital in the Capital Sector. The Imperium is organized into sectors, each governed by a Duke who answers to the Emperor. The Imperial Navy maintains order and defends against external threats. The Imperium has existed for over 1000 years and is in a state of gradual decline.
**Notes:** The Imperium is the primary setting for Classic Traveller. Most adventures take place within Imperial space.

### 1.2 The Spinward Marches
**Title:** The Spinward Marches Sector
**Category:** SETTING_SPINWARD_MARCHES
**Chapter:** Setting
**Page:** 5
**Text:** The Spinward Marches is a sector on the edge of the Third Imperium, bordering the Alien Emperors to the spinward side. It is a frontier region with:
- 1000 star systems arranged in a 10x10 hex grid
- Mix of Imperial, independent, and alien-controlled systems
- Rich in resources but politically unstable
- Home to the continuing adventures published by GDW
- Key worlds include Sol, Nsferu, and many others
**Notes:** The Spinward Marches is the primary adventure sector. The hex map is essential for navigation.

### 1.3 Sector Organization
**Title:** Sector Administration
**Category:** SETTING_IMPERIUM
**Chapter:** Setting
**Page:** 10
**Text:** Each sector is administered by:
- Duke: Sector governor, answers to Emperor
- Subsector Governors: Administer groups of systems
- World Governors: Administer individual worlds
- Imperial Navy: Maintains military presence
- Imperial Customs: Enforces trade laws
- Imperial Intelligence: Monitors security
**Notes:** The chain of command runs from Emperor → Duke → Subsector → World. Corruption is common.

## 2. WORLD DATA

### 2.1 World Types
**Title:** Planet Classification
**Category:** WORLD_GENERATION
**Chapter:** World Data
**Page:** 15
**Text:** Worlds are classified by their UWP characteristics:
- Habitable: Atmosphere 1-5, Size 4-8, Hydro 10-80%
- Uninhabitable: Outside habitable range
- Special: Gas giants, asteroid belts, star systems without planets
- Population levels 0-9 determine civilization density
- Government types 0-9 and A determine political structure
- Law levels 0-9 and None determine freedom vs. control
**Notes:** Only habitable worlds can support human colonies. Technology level determines available equipment.

### 2.2 Starport Classes
**Title:** Starport Facilities
**Category:** WORLD_GENERATION
**Chapter:** World Data
**Page:** 18
**Text:** Starport classes determine facility quality:
- A: Major spaceport, extensive facilities
- B: Large spaceport, good facilities
- C: Average spaceport, standard facilities
- D: Small spaceport, limited facilities
- E: Precarious landing, minimal facilities
- F: Hazardous landing, no facilities
- None: No spaceport, landing impossible or extremely dangerous
**Notes:** Starport class affects ship repair, refueling, and trade costs. A/B ports are preferred.

### 2.3 Technology Levels
**Title:** Level of Technology (TL)
**Category:** WORLD_GENERATION
**Chapter:** World Data
**Page:** 20
**Text:** Technology levels range from 0 to A:
- TL0: Pre-industrial, no advanced technology
- TL1-3: Early industrial, basic machinery
- TL4-5: Industrial, combustion engines, basic electronics
- TL6: Modern, computers, nuclear power
- TL7: Advanced, fusion power, basic spaceflight
- TL8: Advanced spaceflight, lasers
- TL9: Jump drive, full spacefaring
- TL10: Advanced AI, power armor
- TL11: Advanced materials, energy weapons
- TLA: Unknown advanced technology
**Notes:** TL determines what equipment and ships are available. Jump travel requires TL9+.

## 3. NAVADVENTURE: TRILLION CREDIT SQUADRON

### 3.1 Adventure Overview
**Title:** Trillion Credit Squadron Scenario
**Category:** SPACE_COMBAT
**Chapter:** Adventure
**Page:** 1
**Text:** Trillion Credit Squadron is a naval adventure scenario:
- Players command a squadron of Imperial Navy ships
- Mission: intercept and destroy an enemy fleet
- Multiple phases: reconnaissance, engagement, pursuit
- Detailed ship statistics for all vessels
- Pre-generated crew and officer rosters
- Branching outcomes based on player decisions
**Notes:** This is a referee-run scenario. Players should not read this document.

### 3.2 Fleet Composition
**Title:** Squadron Fleet Details
**Category:** SPACE_COMBAT
**Chapter:** Adventure
**Page:** 10
**Text:** The player squadron consists of:
- Flagship: 20,000-ton cruiser
- Destroyers: 2x 5,000-ton destroyers
- Frigates: 4x 2,000-ton frigates
- Total: 7 ships, ~36,000 tons
- Crew: Several hundred officers and enlisted
- Mission duration: Several days of game time
**Notes:** Each ship has detailed statistics including weapons, armor, and crew.

### 3.3 Enemy Forces
**Title:** Enemy Fleet Details
**Category:** SPACE_COMBAT
**Chapter:** Adventure
**Page:** 20
**Text:** The enemy fleet consists of:
- Rebel capital ship: 30,000 tons
- Escort destroyers: 3x 4,000 tons
- Support vessels: 2x 1,000 tons
- Total: 6 ships, ~42,000 tons
- Well-armed and determined
- Commanded by experienced officers
**Notes:** The enemy is slightly stronger than the player squadron. Tactics are key.

## 4. ALIEN RACES

### 4.1 Known Alien Species
**Title:** Alien Races of the Imperium
**Category:** ALIEN_RACES
**Chapter:** Setting
**Page:** 25
**Text:** Known alien species in the Traveller universe:
- Aslan: Cat-like humanoids from the Aslan Compact
- Vargr: Wolf-like humanoids, nomadic raiders
- Insectoids: Insect-like humanoids, hive-minded
- Mellanni: Aquatic humanoids from water worlds
- KigYar: Bird-like humanoids
Alien races have their own cultures, governments, and technologies. They may be allies, neutrals, or enemies of the Imperium.
**Notes:** Alien characters are rare in Classic Traveller. Most aliens are NPCs.

## 5. ENCOUNTER TABLES

### 5.1 Random Encounters
**Title:** Space Encounter Table
**Category:** TABLE_ENCOUNTER
**Chapter:** Tables
**Page:** 30
**Text:** Random space encounters (roll 2D6):
- 2: None
- 3: Derelict ship
- 4: Merchant vessel
- 5: Naval patrol
- 6: Pirate ship
- 7: Scout vessel
- 8: Refugee ship
- 9: Smuggler
- 10: Naval task force
- 11: Alien vessel
- 12: Roll twice, no 12s
**Notes:** Encounters are modified by sector danger level and player actions.

### 5.2 Wildlife Encounters
**Title:** Wildlife Encounter Table
**Category:** TABLE_WILDLIFE
**Chapter:** Tables
**Page:** 35
**Text:** Planetary wildlife encounters (roll 2D6):
- 2-3: None
- 4-5: Herbivores (non-threatening)
- 6-7: Predators (cautious)
- 8-9: Predators (aggressive)
- 10-11: Dangerous predators
- 12: Alpha predator or pack
Wildlife danger scales with planet population and environment.
**Notes:** Wildlife encounters are common on low-population worlds. Players should be prepared.
