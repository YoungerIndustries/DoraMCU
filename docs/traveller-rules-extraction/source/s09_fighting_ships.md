# S09: Fighting Ships — Rules Extraction

## Source
Classic Traveller Supplement 9: Fighting Ships (1981), GDW

## Contents

### Imperial Navy Ship Types (Page 9)
- Scout/Courier (reconnaissance)
- Destroyer Escort (screening)
- Destroyer (anti-fighter)
- Close Escort (convoy protection)
- Fleet Escort (fleet screening)
- Cruiser (independent operations)
- Carrier (fighter operations)
- Dreadnought (capital ship)
- Planetoid Monitor (stationary defense)

### Scout/Courier Class (Page 11)
- 200t, J-2 M-2, 40t fuel
- Crew: 4, Weapons: 1 light laser
- Role: Reconnaissance and courier

### Destroyer Escort Class (Page 14)
- 400t, J-2 M-2, 80t fuel
- Crew: 8, Weapons: 2 light lasers, 1 missile launcher
- Armor: 1 point

### Destroyer Class (Page 16)
- 800t, J-2 M-3, 160t fuel
- Crew: 16, Weapons: 4 light lasers, 2 missile launchers
- Armor: 2 points

### Close Escort Class (Page 17)
- 400t, J-2 M-2, 80t fuel
- Crew: 8, Weapons: 2 light lasers, 1 missile launcher
- Armor: 1 point

### Fleet Escort Class (Page 18)
- 800t, J-2 M-3, 160t fuel
- Crew: 16, Weapons: 4 light lasers, 2 missile launchers
- Armor: 2 points

### Colonial Cruiser Class (Page 19)
- 1600t, J-2 M-2, 320t fuel
- Crew: 32, Weapons: 6 light lasers, 3 missile launchers
- Armor: 3 points

### Light Cruiser Class (Page 28)
- 3200t, J-2 M-3, 640t fuel
- Crew: 64, Weapons: 8 light lasers, 4 missile launchers
- Armor: 4 points

### Strike Cruiser Class (Page 29)
- 3200t, J-2 M-3, 640t fuel
- Crew: 64, Weapons: 10 light lasers, 6 missile launchers
- Armor: 3 points

### Heavy Cruiser Class (Page 32)
- 6400t, J-2 M-2, 1280t fuel
- Crew: 128, Weapons: 12 light lasers, 6 missile launchers
- Armor: 6 points

### Light Carrier Class (Page 34)
- 12800t, J-2 M-2, 2560t fuel
- Crew: 256, Fighters: 12
- Weapons: 8 light lasers, 4 missile launchers
- Armor: 4 points

### Fleet Carrier Class (Page 36)
- 25600t, J-2 M-2, 5120t fuel
- Crew: 512, Fighters: 24
- Weapons: 12 light lasers, 6 missile launchers
- Armor: 6 points

### Dreadnought Class (Page 38)
- 100000t, J-2 M-1, 20000t fuel
- Crew: 2000, Weapons: 20 light lasers, 10 missile launchers
- Armor: 10 points

### Planetoid Monitor (Page 44)
- Stationary defense platform
- Multiple laser batteries and missile launchers
- Heavy armor, cannot jump

### Imperial Naval Bases (Page 6)
- **Naval Bases**: Full facilities for fleet operations
- **Naval Depots**: Limited resupply and repair
- **Way Stations**: Minimal emergency facilities
